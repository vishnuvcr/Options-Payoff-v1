# Interim corrected H1 selection behaviour

Completed years 2021, 2024 and 2026 contain 54 realized H1 trades under the exact 17-strike rule. **ATM (shift 0) was selected zero times.** The observed selections are concentrated at the outer strike shifts, especially -400 (11), -350 (10), +350 (9), -250 (7) and +400 (7).

This is descriptive selection behaviour only. It is not used as a filter and does not alter the preregistered strategy.
